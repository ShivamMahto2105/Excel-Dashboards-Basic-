# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard (FNP)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project showcases an **interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard** built to analyze sales performance for **FNP (Ferns N Petals)**.  
The dashboard provides insights into **orders, revenue, customer behavior, delivery timelines, and regional performance**, helping businesses make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze **total orders** and **total revenue**
- Track **average delivery time** between order and delivery
- Understand **customer spending behavior**
- Identify **top products, categories, occasions, and cities**
- Monitor **monthly and hourly sales trends**

---

## 📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
- **Total Orders:** 1000  
- **Total Revenue:** ₹35,20,984  
- **Average Days Between Order & Delivery:** 5.53 days  
- **Average Customer Spending:** ₹3,520.98  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Insights

### 1️⃣ Revenue by Occasion
- Highest revenue from **Anniversary** and **Raksha Bandhan**
- Lower sales during **Diwali** and **Valentine’s Day**

### 2️⃣ Revenue by Category
- **Colors** and **Soft Toys** contribute the most revenue
- **Mugs** and **Plants** have comparatively lower sales

### 3️⃣ Revenue by Order Hour
- Peak revenue observed during **evening hours**
- Suggests higher customer engagement later in the day

### 4️⃣ Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue spikes during **February** and **August**
- Seasonal demand visible around festivals and special occasions

### 5️⃣ Top Products by Revenue
- Consistent high performance from top 5 products
- Revenue fluctuation indicates varying customer preferences

### 6️⃣ Top 10 Cities by Orders
- Cities like **Imphal**, **Dhanbad**, and **Kavali** lead in order volume
- Useful for regional marketing and logistics planning
