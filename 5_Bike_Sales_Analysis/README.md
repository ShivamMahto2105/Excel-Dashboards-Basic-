# 🏍 Indian Bike Sales Analysis Dashboard

This interactive Excel dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of Indian bike sales performance, built to analyze pricing trends, resale value, fuel distribution, and regional demand patterns.

---

## 📊 Key Features

- **Interactive Filters:** Easily slice data by:
  - Seller Type (Dealer / Individual)
  - Registration Year (2015–2024)
  - Brand Selection

- **Data Insights:**
  - Brand-wise average bike price
  - Original vs Resale price comparison
  - Fuel type distribution
  - Bike price distribution across city tiers
  - Top 10 bike models analysis
  - State-wise bike sales performance

## 🔍 Insights Gained

- Bajaj and Yamaha show competitive average pricing among major brands.
- Clear depreciation gap observed between original and resale prices.
- Fuel distribution shows balanced market segmentation.
- Tier 3 cities show slightly higher pricing compared to Tier 2.
- Maharashtra and Punjab demonstrate strong sales performance.

---

## 📥 How to Use

1. Open the `.xlsx` file in Microsoft Excel.
2. Use the slicers on the left panel to filter by brand, seller type, and year.
3. Observe dynamic updates in charts and KPI cards.
